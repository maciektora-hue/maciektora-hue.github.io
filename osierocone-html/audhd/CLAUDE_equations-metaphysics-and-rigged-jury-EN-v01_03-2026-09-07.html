Principle of stationary action

δS = 0

The actual path of a system is the one for which a small variation of the entire trajectory does not change the action to first order. Not the “shortest” and not always the “smallest”: stationary. Classical physics pretends to be a local causal machine while underneath it is describing the whole history at once.

In the quantum version, the insolence becomes explicit:

K(B,A) = ∫ 𝒟[x] · eiS[x]/ℏ

The particle takes every possible path. Each gets a phase; most histories cancel one another out. Near the classical path, the phases become unusually cooperative and stop getting in one another's way.

4

Bell–CHSH and the Tsirelson bound

This is an experimental detector for metaphysical fraud. It tests whether the world can be a local machine in which particles have their answers printed before measurement.

S = E(A₀B₀) + E(A₀B₁) + E(A₁B₀) − E(A₁B₁)

If Alice and Bob receive only local, prewritten instructions with outcomes ±1, then for every pair:

A₀(B₀ + B₁) + A₁(B₀ − B₁) = ±2

One bracket must be zero; the other is ±2. After averaging, we get the classical ceiling:

|S| ≤ 2

Quantum mechanics predicts, and experiment finds, more:

|S| ≤ 2√2

Arithmetic would allow 4, but the quantum world stops at 2√2. It is more strongly correlated than a local classical filing cabinet of pre-existing answers permits, but less strongly than pure logic plus the ban on faster-than-light communication would allow.

You cannot use this to send an instantaneous message: individual outcomes remain random, and the correlation appears only after they are compared. Nature cheats classical intuition, but it will not let us turn the trick into quantum WhatsApp.

Bell does not simply prove that “reality does not exist.” It throws a whole package out of the window: locality, independent measurement choice, and the classical idea of pre-existing answers. This is not a description of one object. It is an inequality used to experimentally shoot down an entire class of possible realities.

Uncertainty relation

σAσB ≥ ½ |⟨[A,B]⟩|
Δx · Δp ≥ ℏ/2

This is not about a lab technician's shaky hand or a microscope that was too cheap. A quantum state cannot simultaneously possess arbitrarily sharp position and momentum. The limitation lives in the noncommutativity of the operators, which means it sits in the geometry of the theory itself.

Dirac equation

(iℏγμ∂μ − mc)ψ = 0

The attempt to reconcile quantum mechanics with special relativity produced spin almost for free, then spat out negative energies. Instead of sweeping them under the rug, physics took them seriously and found antimatter. Mathematical elegance invoiced reality; reality paid in positrons.

10

Wheeler–DeWitt

ĤΨ = 0

An equation meant to describe the entire Universe, and there is no time in it. Not “time flows relatively.” Not “time is strange.” The fucker has been deleted from the equation.

The Universe as a whole may be timeless, while our “happening” may emerge from correlations among its parts. A clock would then not measure some external river of time; it would simply change relative to the rest of the world. The whole cosmos stands still while everything inside it runs.

This is not the confirmed last word of physics, but it is the central monster of canonical quantum gravity. For the question alone, “what if time is a property of an observer inside the Universe rather than of the Universe itself?”, it deserves a place in the final.
