Song
analysis.

For several years, every song I like has gone into one continuous “Liked songs” list rather than being scattered across many separate lists. The collection preserves the order in which songs were liked, giving it a natural time axis. Once it became large enough, it turned into useful material for analysis.

Two branches of the same library. One looks at lyrics, meaning and change over time. The other looks at audio features and the structure of sound. The playlist viewer exposes the data layer underneath.
